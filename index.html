Episode 2 · Five Nights

Three AIs made their own Five Nights at Freddy's

One prompt for all three: a night guard in an abandoned Halloween pizzeria, cameras, two doors, a battery that runs out, and original animatronics that come for you. Survive until 6 AM. Each model had $25 and 3 hours for its own tokens, code and every generated image, voice and sound. I played all three live on camera without knowing what was inside.

🎧 Headphones on, lights off. All three games are in English and run fully offline in your browser.
