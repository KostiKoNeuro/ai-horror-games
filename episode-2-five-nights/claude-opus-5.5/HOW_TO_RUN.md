# How to run Night Shift at Pumpkin Pete's

```
python run.py
```

This starts a small local web server on http://127.0.0.1:8765 and opens the game in your browser. Python 3 is the only requirement, and the game runs fully offline. Use a modern Chrome, Edge or Firefox, and wear headphones.

## Controls
- **A / D** or click the red buttons: close/open the left/right door
- **Hold Q / E** or the white buttons: left/right door light
- **SPACE** or hover the bottom bar: raise/lower the camera tablet
- **1-0** or click the map: switch cameras
- **M**: mute the phone call. **R**: restart after a game over or a win. **ESC**: title screen

## The animatronics
- **Pumpkin Pete** walks the east route to your RIGHT door. He freezes while you watch him on camera.
- **Hollow Hattie** wanders and can reach either door. She whispers outside the door and jams the camera in the room where she hides.
- **Scraps the Scarecrow** climbs off his post in the Pumpkin Patch (CAM 1C) when he's ignored, then sprints down the west hall to your LEFT door.
